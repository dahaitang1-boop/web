Storm & cold — quick protocol

  • Thunderstorm: off ridges, summits, and cable sections immediately; away from summit crosses and lone boulders; crouch on the pack, feet together; spread out if in a group
  • Fresh snow / fog on a pass: do not cross — return to the last hut; the Hüttenwirt's advice overrides the plan
  • Hypothermia signs (stumbling, slurring, confusion): stop, insulate, sugar + warm drink, call 140 early — do not push on
  • Injured & immobile: insulate from the ground first, then call; if no signal, distress signal + send the strongest walker to the nearest hut
