Go / No-Go rules

  • Stage 2 (11–12 h) — the hardest day; go only on a stable forecast, alpine start 06:00. Plan B: descend Gamshütte→Ginzling ~2 h → Bus 4102 → Schlegeis, walk to Dominikushütte. Same bed, half the day. Check the 4102 timetable the evening before.
  • Stage 3 combined day (3,134 m) — cross Schönbichler Horn before midday, 07:00 start. Storm risk before Furtschaglhaus → wait there or turn back to Schlegeis. Bad forecast from the start → Plan B valley route: Bus 4102 → Breitlahner → Zemmgrund → Berliner Hütte (T2, ~7 h, no pass). Decide at Dominikushütte the evening before.
  • Stage 7 (Königsetappe) — no escape, no water en route. Bad forecast ⇒ do not start; descend Stilluptal instead (Grüne-Wand → WL2/taxi).
  • Fresh snow above ~2,700 m — passes (Schönbichler, Mörchenscharte, Lapenscharte) become hazardous; ask each Hüttenwirt every evening.
  • Afternoon thunderstorms (Gewitter) are the default summer hazard — start early, be off ridges by early afternoon.

    Distress signal — no phone signal

    • Call for help — 6 signals/min (whistle, torch, waving) · 1 min pause · repeat
    • Answer — 3 signals/min
    • Helicopter Y/N — both arms up = YES, need help · one arm up + one down = NO
    • 112 tip — no bars on your network? Dial 112 anyway, it hops to any available carrier. Flight mode between attempts saves battery.

    Storm & cold — quick protocol

    • Thunderstorm — off ridges, summits and cable sections immediately; away from summit crosses and lone boulders; crouch on the pack, feet together; spread out if in a group
    • Fresh snow / fog on a pass — do not cross, return to the last hut; the Hüttenwirt's read overrides the plan
    • Hypothermia signs (stumbling, slurring, confusion) — stop, insulate, sugar + warm drink, call 140 early, do not push on
    • Injured & immobile — insulate from the ground first, then call; if no signal, distress signal + send the strongest walker to the nearest hut
